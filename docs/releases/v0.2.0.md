OpenFile v0.2.0 adds training to the database: spaced-repetition decks, mate puzzles built from your own games, replayed training questions and guided game review. It also brings deeper search, more engine tools and more of the annotation format.

## Highlights

### Training

- **Spaced Repetition.** Turn a PGN file, or the games of a database list, into a deck of lines and study it day by day.
  - You play your side's moves and the opponent's replies play themselves. New lines are first shown with arrows.
  - A mistake brings a line back soon, and lines you know come back at growing intervals: Anki-style scheduling with Again, Hard, Good and Easy.
  - Daily limits, deck statistics, and updating a deck from its source without losing progress.
- **Build Mate Training.** From the games in a list, builds "Mate in 1" to "Mate in 5" databases and a "Mate chains" database.
  - Each puzzle is a forced mate in exactly N with a single mating first move. Mates in 1 and 2 are solved exactly; 3 to 5 are checked with your engine.
  - Puzzles are balanced across the combinations of pieces that give the mate.
  - The chains show the same mate one move further back each time.
- **Replay Training.** Practise the training questions saved in a game, or in the selected games of a list, on a board with the answer hidden. Progress is kept between sessions.
- **Training annotations.** Training questions are read, written, shown in the notation and edited (Training Annotation, Ctrl+Alt+M).
- **Guided game review.** After engine analysis, a review shows estimated accuracy for both sides, a move-quality timeline and the critical positions. You can then practise those positions until they are solved.
- **Repertoire.**
  - A White and Black repertoire workspace in the game window: saved choices, a preferred move and notes, coverage against the reference database, and practice.
  - Generate Repertoire and the Repertoire Report.

### Search

- **Material.** Piece ranges per side, endgame presets, material balance, colour reversal, a move window and variations.
- **Ending tab.**
  - Piece counts in the final position, optionally for either colour.
  - **Mate by**: games that end in mate delivered by a chosen combination of pieces, such as queen and knight.
  - On the game data tab, **Equal pieces** finds games that end with equal material, with bishops and knights counted alike.
- **Refine results.** Search all games, only within the current results, or add to them.
- **Reference pane.** Filter the reference moves by Elo, year or a good-game rule, and compare a player's choices as White or Black.

### Analysis and engines

- **Engine pane commands:** Analyse Threat, Lock Engine, Next Best and Search Better.
- **Evaluation profile:** a curve of the game with its critical moves, and Instant Analysis.
- **Centipawn Analysis** of a game, and of a whole database list with per-player statistics and CSV export.
- **Engine history:** a kibitzer pane can show every change of the engine's best move with its depth, score and time.
- **Clearer engine errors:** a clear explanation when macOS blocks a downloaded engine, or when a folder is chosen as an engine.
- **Playing style report** (partial): two of its eight dimensions are fully computed so far.

### Databases, annotations and the board

- **Export PGN** of the selected games of a list.
- **Database start screen:** Tile, Details, Symbols and Small Symbols views, database properties, and **Recover readable games** from a damaged `.2cbh` database into a safe copy.
- **More annotation types:** move medals, move times, diagrams, pawn structures, piece paths, critical positions and stored evaluations are read, written and shown.
- **Drag and drop pieces** with the mouse or touch, on game, practice and training boards.

## Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS, Apple Silicon or Intel | `.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11 (x64) | `-setup.exe`, or `.msi` |
| Linux x64 | `.AppImage`, `.deb` or `.rpm` |

No engine is bundled. Add one with **Add Kibitzer** (Ctrl+K); on macOS, `brew install stockfish` installs Stockfish at `/opt/homebrew/bin/stockfish`. Build Mate Training needs an engine for mates in 3 to 5.

## Installing: the builds are not signed yet

This release is not signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your system warns you the first time you open it.

**macOS.** Open the `.dmg` and drag **OpenFile** into **Applications**. On the first launch, macOS says it cannot verify the app:
1. Click **Done** (not Move to Trash).
2. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down to **Security**, and click **Open Anyway** next to "OpenFile was blocked". Confirm with your password.

You only need to do this once. On macOS 14 and earlier, right-clicking the app and choosing **Open** also works. If macOS instead says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", it is the download quarantine, not a broken file. Run this once in Terminal, then open the app normally:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/OpenFile.app"
```

**Windows.** If SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info › Run anyway**. The app uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 10 and 11 already have.

**Linux.** Make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x OpenFile_*.AppImage`) and run it, or install the package (`sudo apt install ./OpenFile_*.deb`, or `sudo dnf install ./OpenFile-*.rpm`). It needs WebKitGTK 4.1: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 or later.

## Known limitations

- The Windows and Linux builds are made automatically and have not yet been tried on real machines. Please report anything that goes wrong.
- Classic `.cbh` databases open read-only; the app writes the `.2cbh` format only.
- Build Mate Training checks one position at a time with a single engine, so a large build takes a while. Stop keeps the puzzles found so far.
- Spaced Repetition has no card browser or undo yet.
- Not there yet: printing and publishing, correspondence chess, and vendor online services. See the [feature checklist](https://github.com/d4nu-00/openfile/blob/main/feature_checklist.md) and [compatibility notes](https://github.com/d4nu-00/openfile/blob/main/docs/compatibility.md).
